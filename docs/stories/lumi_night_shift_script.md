# Lumi – Night Shift (script v2, ~90 s, photoreal + colored-pencil daydream)

Status: **v2 (Oct 18, 2:40 PM ET)** after the v7 review: new story beats, no cat, original dialogue in
OpenSoul's own deadpan tone (no quoted lines), one locked store set (`production/setbible/`). v1 is in git history.
All characters are original OpenSoul characters (Lumi, Mio, Ren). Store name invented (Hoshikage Mart); no real
brands, logos or readable text; the coffee can has an invented, unreadable label.


**Hook:** a centuries-old elf works the 3 AM shift at a Tokyo konbini. A tipsy couple wants canned coffee. She has opinions.
**Tone:** quiet, dry, warm. Lumi never raises her voice; the joke is how seriously she takes coffee and how casually she
mentions time. Mio and Ren are giggly-tipsy and sweet, talking over each other. No cat. One locked store set.

**How the tone works:** short sentences, long pauses, no exclamation marks for Lumi; time is
casual for her ("sometime later" means decades); Mio is the sensible one even when tipsy; Ren is
earnest and a beat behind. All lines are original.
**Voices (Orpheus, Apache-2.0):** Lumi = tara, Mio = leah, Ren = zac. 3 takes per line, picked by speech-to-text accuracy plus pitch movement.

## Production notes
- **Set:** every interior shot is edited from the locked master plate (no cat), never re-imagined.
- **Lip-sync:** Lumi's lines: medium shot, LTX a2v + LatentSync mouth pass. Mio/Ren: on-screen when one face holds, otherwise off-screen.
- **Can:** a slim metal beverage can with a pull tab, label = invented shapes, no letters.
- **Daydream:** colored-pencil anime style (owner's style ref, style only), warm sunset palette, visible grain.
- **Ears:** subtle; hidden by hair in the ending.

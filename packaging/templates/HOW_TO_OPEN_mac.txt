How to open OpenSoul on a Mac (Apple chip, macOS 13 or newer)

1. Disk image (.dmg): double-click it. Drag OpenSoul onto the Applications folder in the window.
   Zip: if the zip is not open yet, double-click it. Then drag OpenSoul into your Applications folder.
   If you open OpenSoul from Downloads, it asks one time to move to Applications. It does not move without your click.
2. First time only: right-click (or Control-click) OpenSoul. Choose Open. Then click Open again.

   If your Mac shows only "Done" or "Move to Trash", open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
   Scroll to OpenSoul. Click "Open Anyway". Confirm.
   Your Mac shows this message because OpenSoul is not from the App Store.
3. The first time, OpenSoul needs about one minute to get ready.
   Then OpenSoul opens in its own window. Its icon is in the Dock and in Launchpad.
   If the window cannot open, your web browser shows OpenSoul.
4. Pick "Try it now" to explore with sample results.
   Or pick "Connect my studio". Type the address of your studio, for example http://my-studio.local:8188.
   Then click Test connection. "Is this safe?" on that page tells you what OpenSoul sends, and where.
5. To close OpenSoul, close the window or press Command-Q.
   To open it again, click OpenSoul in the Dock, in Launchpad or in Applications.

Still blocked? Open Terminal. Run this command: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/OpenSoul.app
OpenSoul keeps your work in: your home folder > Library > Application Support > OpenSoul.

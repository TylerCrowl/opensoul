How to open OpenSoul on Linux

1. Unpack the archive in your home folder: tar xzf OpenSoul-*.tar.gz
2. Double-click OpenSoul/OpenSoul. Or run ./OpenSoul/OpenSoul in a terminal.
   Your browser opens the welcome page.
3. Optional: run ./OpenSoul/install-desktop-entry.sh one time. This adds OpenSoul to your applications menu.
4. Pick "Try it now" to use Preview mode. You do not need a graphics card.
   Or pick "Connect my studio". Then type the address of your studio.
5. To close OpenSoul, click Quit in the bottom-right corner of the page.
   You can also run ./OpenSoul/OpenSoul stop.

OpenSoul keeps your work in ~/.local/share/opensoul.

How to open OpenSoul on Windows (64-bit)

1. Right-click the zip. Choose Extract All. Pick a folder, for example Documents.
2. Open the OpenSoul folder. Double-click OpenSoul.cmd. Your browser opens the welcome page.

   Windows can show "Windows protected your PC". We did not sign OpenSoul yet.
   If you see this message, click More info. Then click Run anyway.
3. Pick "Try it now" to use Preview mode. You do not need a graphics card.
   Or pick "Connect my studio". Then type the address of your studio.
4. To close OpenSoul, click Quit in the bottom-right corner of the page.
5. Tip: for a desktop icon, right-click OpenSoul.cmd. Choose Send to > Desktop (create shortcut).

OpenSoul keeps your work in %LOCALAPPDATA%\OpenSoul.

# Juno Pell (sample character)

**All rights reserved until the project decides.** Not licensed under Apache-2.0 (see `/ASSETS_LICENSE`).

An original AI character made for OpenSoul's sample music video *Spin Cycle*. She is an adult (early thirties).
She was made **from text only, with open models**: no photo of any person, no reference image and no closed
generator. Preview mode uses her pictures and clips as samples (`assets/demo_preview/`).

| File | What | How it was made |
|---|---|---|
| `juno_ref_00.jpg` | Master picture (front) | Z-Image-Turbo (Apache-2.0), text only, seed 303 of 101/202/303/404, picked by eye. Re-encoded to 640×960 JPEG. |
| `juno_character_sheet_v1.jpg` | Sheet with 4 views | The master plus three Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 (Apache-2.0) views made from it (three-quarter, full body, wardrobe B), side by side. |
| `character.json` | Prompt lock, persona, look | Written for the sample |

Full provenance (models, revisions, licenses, source checksums): `assets/demo_preview/PROVENANCE.md`.

        No shots yet
        
          
        Each shot is one moment of your story. Add them one by one, or paste a script and we’ll split it into shots for you.
        
          
        
            
            
          
        
        
        
        
          
          
          
          
        
          Click a shot’s name to rename it. Drag to reorder (or Alt + ← / →). Open a shot to pick its cast. You can also make a start frame and takes there.
          
        
          
          
          Stages
          A stage sheet keeps a place looking the same in every shot: a master plate plus a few angles.

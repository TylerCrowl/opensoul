# The Roast Date
**Runtime:** ~14s · **Format:** 9:16 · **Character:** Lumi

## Spoken script
"This was roasted eleven days ago.
It is at its best. So am I.

Tip: look for the roast date, not the best-before.
Two to three weeks off roast is the sweet spot."

## Useful tip
Buy whole beans with a printed roast date and brew them roughly one to three weeks after roasting; grind just before brewing.

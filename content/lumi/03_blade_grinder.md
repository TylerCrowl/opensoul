# Not the Blade Grinder
**Runtime:** ~13s · **Format:** 9:16 · **Character:** Lumi

## Spoken script
"I have watched empires fall.
I will not watch you use a blade grinder.

Tip: a burr grinder makes every grain the same size,
so the cup tastes even, not bitter and sour at once."

## Useful tip
Burr grinders give an even grind size; blade grinders chop unevenly, which over- and under-extracts in the same cup.

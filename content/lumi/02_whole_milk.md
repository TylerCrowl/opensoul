# Whole Milk, Sixty-Five Degrees
**Runtime:** ~15s · **Format:** 9:16 · **Character:** Lumi

## Spoken script
"Whole milk. Sixty-five degrees.
Anything hotter and you are drinking an apology.

Tip: stop steaming when the jug is
just too hot to hold. The milk stays sweet."

## Useful tip
Steam milk to about 60–65 °C; past roughly 70 °C it loses sweetness and the foam breaks down.

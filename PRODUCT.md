# OpenSoul — Product context

## One-liner
OpenSoul helps anyone create a consistent AI character, make matching photos, and turn a short script into a talking vertical video — entirely on their own computer.

## Who it's for
Non-technical creators and homestead / brand storytellers who want a friendly character that looks like the same person across scenes. They should not need to know model names, SDKs, or ComfyUI.

## Jobs to be done
1. Create a character from a few photos (or try the Lumi demo).
2. Make scene photos that stay on-character, with a clear pass/fail check.
3. Make a short talking video from a script.

## Success
A first-time visitor can open the app, try the demo, and understand the three steps without reading docs. Technical details stay in Advanced.

## Constraints
- Contest entry uses open-weight models via Comfy locally; UI must not expose that jargon on the main path.
- Lumi demo assets are all-rights-reserved; Pixel is the redistributable example.
- Dry-run mode works without a GPU for demos and screenshots.

## Assumptions (brief-inferred)
- Operate-mode product UI, not a marketing landing page.
- en-US first; light theme preferred for demos/screenshots.
